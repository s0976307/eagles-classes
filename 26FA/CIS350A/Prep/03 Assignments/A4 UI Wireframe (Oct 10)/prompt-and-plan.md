### Wireframe vs. mock-up vs. prototype — from your own Ch. 4 notes (2026-09-28)

The single most likely way to lose points here is building the wrong artifact.

| Artifact | Focus | The question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| **Wireframe** | Layout + functionality | *"Where does everything go?"* |
| Mock-up | Finished appearance | *"What will it look like?"* |
| Prototype | Interaction, limited function | *"How does it behave?"* |

**A wireframe is a blueprint.** Structure, content blocks, controls, labels, and what each
region is *for*. Not colors, not fonts, not photography, not brand styling.

**Over-polishing a wireframe into a mock-up is a real way to lose marks** — it answers a
question the assignment did not ask. Greyscale boxes and honest labels are the deliverable.
